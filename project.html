Annual Operating Budgeting for 3 North Louisiana Hospitals

Assisting regional finance leadership with the development, variance analysis, and review of comprehensive multi-facility operating budgets.

Project Overview

During my summer internship at Ochsner Health System in Shreveport, LA, I collaborated with the finance team to build and validate annual operational budgets across three major hospital campuses in North Louisiana. This process required reconciling prior-year actuals, identifying departmental cost trends, and modeling projected expenditures.

Core Responsibilities & Methodology

  • Strata Financial Modeling: Utilized Strata financial planning software to review cost center allocations, track baseline operating expenses, and input adjusted fiscal targets for clinical and administrative departments.
  • Variance Analysis: Compared historical financial trends against departmental forecasts to identify budget discrepancies, seasonal fluctuations in patient volume, and supply chain cost shifts.
  • Departmental Rollups: Assisted in preparing consolidated financial summary schedules across all three regional facilities to provide hospital executives with a clear picture of net operating margins.

Strata Microsoft Excel Variance Analysis Financial Modeling Healthcare Accounting

Workforce Analytics • Workday Healthcare Operations

Hospital Labor & Payroll Cost Analysis

Tracking staffing costs, overtime patterns, and labor productivity metrics to support efficient clinical department scheduling.

Project Overview

Labor constitutes the single largest operational expenditure in healthcare systems. In this initiative, I extracted and evaluated large payroll datasets from Workday to give hospital leadership actionable visibility into staffing expenditures, overtime hours, and FTE (Full-Time Equivalent) variance across nursing and operational units.

Core Responsibilities & Methodology

  • Workday Data Extraction: Queried payroll registries, hour classifications (regular vs. overtime vs. on-call), and agency nursing invoices to assemble unified labor reports.
  • Overtime & Premium Pay Tracking: Built specialized Microsoft Excel tracking sheets with dynamic formulas and PivotTables to highlight shift patterns causing unplanned overtime spikes.
  • Productivity & Staffing Metrics: Evaluated hours worked per unit of service (UOS) to help department managers align shift scheduling with real-time patient census.

Emergency Department Analytics & Performance Metrics

Extracting healthcare KPIs through OBIEE to evaluate patient flow, departmental throughput, and resource allocation in the ED.

Project Overview

Emergency Departments demand rapid operational adaptation to fluctuating patient volumes. Using OBIEE (Oracle Business Intelligence Enterprise Edition), I extracted clinical and throughput metrics to evaluate peak volume hours, admission rates, and patient turnaround metrics.

Core Responsibilities & Methodology

  • OBIEE Healthcare Querying: Filtered and exported high-volume hospital datasets capturing patient arrivals, acuity classifications, and length-of-stay milestones.
  • Reporting & Spreadsheets: Transformed raw database extracts into structured Excel dashboards visualizing day-of-week volume trends, average patient wait times, and bed utilization rates.
  • Interdepartmental Collaboration: Partnered with clinical and finance managers to provide reliable data that helps balance nurse staffing with incoming ED patient volume surges.
